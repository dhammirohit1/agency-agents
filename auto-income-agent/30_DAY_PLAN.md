## 🚨 Warning Signs & Pivots

### If No Revenue After Week 2:
1. **Reassess pricing** - Are you too expensive/cheap?
2. **Improve proposals** - Get feedback, A/B test
3. **Try different platforms** - Fiverr, Freelancer, LinkedIn
4. **Offer promotions** - Discount for first clients
5. **Manual outreach** - Don't rely only on automation

### If Trading Losing Money:
1. **Return to testnet** - Practice more
2. **Reduce position sizes** - Risk less per trade
3. **Change strategy** - Try different indicators
4. **Pause trading** - Focus on other streams
5. **Get education** - Study trading strategies

### If Content Not Growing:
1. **Analyze competitors** - What's working for them?
2. **Improve hooks** - First 3 seconds are critical
3. **Post more consistently** - Algorithm favors consistency
4. **Engage with audience** - Reply to all comments
5. **Try different formats** - Shorts, reels, long-form
