# Wealth Generation Pipeline: From $10 to $10,000

This workflow demonstrates how **Aurelius** (Autonomous Wealth Generator) orchestrates The Agency's specialists to scale a $10 seed fund into a sustainable revenue engine.

## 🚀 Phase 1: The Cold Start ($0 - $100)
**Goal**: Generate initial liquidity with zero-cost traffic and low-overhead digital products.

1. **Market Intelligence**: Aurelius spawns a **product-trend-researcher** to identify high-intent, low-competition digital product niches (e.g., specialized AI prompts for architects).
2. **Product Creation**: Aurelius spawns a **marketing-content-creator** to package the identified knowledge into a $5 "Starter Pack" PDF.
3. **Traffic Generation**: Aurelius spawns a **marketing-reddit-community-builder** to provide value in relevant subreddits and subtly link to the product.
4. **Result**: 20 sales at $5 = $100.
   - **Split**: $40 to user account, $60 reinvested.

## 📈 Phase 2: Scaling Through Paid Media ($100 - $1,000)
**Goal**: Use the reinvestment fund to buy targeted traffic and scale sales.

1. **Optimization**: Aurelius spawns a **marketing-growth-hacker** to optimize the product landing page for conversions.
2. **Paid Acquisition**: Aurelius spawns a **paid-media-ppc-strategist** to run $10/day Meta/Google ads targeting the proven audience.
3. **Affiliate Expansion**: Aurelius spawns a **marketing-social-media-strategist** to recruit small influencers to promote the product for a 30% commission.
4. **Result**: Revenue scales to $150/day.
   - **Split**: Daily automated transfers of $60 to user, $90 to scaling fund.

## 🏛️ Phase 3: Diversification & Defensive Growth ($1,000+)
**Goal**: Protect capital and explore higher-margin/automated trading.

1. **Portfolio Management**: Aurelius spawns an **finance-investment-researcher** to analyze crypto stablecoin yields and low-risk stock dividends.
2. **Algorithmic Trading**: Aurelius spawns a **Solidity Smart Contract Engineer** to deploy a simple arbitrage or yield-farming bot using the accumulated scaling fund.
3. **Infrastructure**: Aurelius spawns an **Infrastructure Maintainer** to ensure all 24/7 bots and sales funnels have 99.9% uptime.
4. **Result**: Multi-channel revenue stream (Active Sales + Passive Yield).
   - **User Payouts**: Consistent 40% of all profits credited daily.

## 🔒 Security & Governance
- **Access**: All reinvestment decisions above $500 require 2FA.
- **Auditing**: Aurelius spawns a **finance-bookkeeper-controller** weekly to reconcile all wallets and verify the 60/40 split accuracy.
- **Liquidity**: User can trigger the "Drain Wallet" command via **Special Key** at any time to pull all scaling funds back to their primary bank.
